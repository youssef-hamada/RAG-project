# Travel & Expense Policy

## General

Only reasonable, necessary, and business-related expenses are reimbursable.
Receipts are required for any single expense over $25.

## Meals

Meal reimbursement is capped at $75 per day while traveling for business.
Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.
Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
Business class requires VP approval.

## Hotels

Hotel reimbursement is capped at $250 per night in tier-1 cities and $180 per night elsewhere.

## Submission

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
Reports submitted after 60 days may be denied.
