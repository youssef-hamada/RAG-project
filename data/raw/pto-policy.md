# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 1.25 days of PTO per month (15 days per year).
Accrual begins on the first day of employment.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year.
Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31.

## Requesting PTO

PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance via the HR portal.
Requests longer than 5 consecutive days require manager approval at least 10 business days in advance.

## Payout

PTO is not paid out on termination, except where required by state law.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Full-time employees receive 7 sick days per year.
Sick leave may be used for personal illness or to care for an immediate family member.
