# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees in good standing after 90 days of employment may request remote work.
Approval is at the discretion of the direct manager and HR.

## Work-from-home days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
At least 2 days per week must be on-site at the assigned office.

## International remote work

Working from outside the country of employment is not permitted without prior written approval from HR and Legal.
Maximum duration for approved international work is 30 calendar days per year.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a one-time $300 home-office stipend.
Internet costs are not reimbursed.

## Core hours

Regardless of location, all employees must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 local company time.
