# Information Security Policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 180 days.
Reusing any of the last 5 passwords is prohibited.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on all company systems.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled.
Personal devices may access email only through the company MDM profile.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Confidential and Restricted data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.

## Incident reporting

Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@company.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## Phishing

Employees must report suspected phishing emails using the "Report Phishing" button in the mail client.
