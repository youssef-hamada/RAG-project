# Code of Conduct

## Respect in the workplace

Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are prohibited.
Reports may be made anonymously through the ethics hotline.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment or financial interest that could conflict with company duties.
Disclosures are reviewed by the Compliance team within 10 business days.

## Gifts

Employees may accept gifts valued at $50 or less from vendors or partners.
Gifts above $50 must be declined or donated.

## Confidentiality

Confidential information must not be disclosed to third parties without a signed NDA.

## Social media

Employees must not post confidential company information on social media.
Personal opinions expressed online must be clearly identified as the employee's own.
